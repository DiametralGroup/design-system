Timeline

A vertical event list: a 1px rule runs down the left as a rail, each item hangs a small square dot on it, and stacks a faint timestamp, a title, and supporting body text. Status families recolor the dot — no radius, no shadow.

Items align to the right of the rail; the last item drops its bottom spacing.

React

The <Timeline> binding is parts, which is what lets an item hold a Card or a diff rather than a string. Tone rides on the item, and data-state fills or outlines the indicator with it.

<Timeline> 
import {
  Timeline,
  TimelineItem,
  TimelineIndicator,
  TimelineContent,
  TimelineTitle,
  TimelineTime,
  TimelineDescription,
} from "@diametral/design-system/react";

{/* Tone rides on the item, not the indicator: a timeline's semantics are
    per-event. data-state fills the indicator with that tone. */}
<Timeline>
  <TimelineItem tone="success" data-state="completed">
    <TimelineIndicator />
    <TimelineContent>
      <TimelineTitle>Matrix published</TimelineTitle>
      <TimelineTime>09:42</TimelineTime>
      <TimelineDescription>All 1,204 rates synced.</TimelineDescription>
    </TimelineContent>
  </TimelineItem>
  <TimelineItem>
    <TimelineIndicator />
    <TimelineContent>
      <TimelineTitle>Draft created</TimelineTitle>
      <TimelineTime>Yesterday</TimelineTime>
    </TimelineContent>
  </TimelineItem>
</Timeline>
